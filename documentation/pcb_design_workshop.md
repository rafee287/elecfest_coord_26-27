# PCB DESIGN CHALLENGE  

## Event Overview
* **Target Audience:** 1st to 4th-year undergraduates and professionals looking to learn PCB design.
* **Event Aim:** Help participants design their first multilayer PCB featuring both low-current and high-current sections, while teaching industry-standard best practices.
* **Sponsors:** DigiKey, Mouser Electronics, PCBWay, JLCPCB

## Event Prerequisites
* **Hardware:** Laptop (a physical mouse is highly recommended).
* **Software:** Pre-installation of KiCad (or chosen EDA tool).
* **Knowledge:** Basic understanding of electric circuits and introductory electromagnetics.

---

## Event Timeline & Flow

### Session 1: Fundamentals & Schematic Design
* **09:00 - 09:45 | Introduction to PCBs**
  * What is a PCB and why do we need them?
  * Physical structure and layers of a PCB.
  * Core terminology: traces (width/thickness), layers, footprints, component types, and ground planes.
  * Overview of the PCB manufacturing process.
* **09:45 - 10:00 | Project Brief & Software Setup**
  * Description of the target board (low-current vs. high-current sections).
  * Introduction to the EDA software interface.
  * Booting up the software and initial Q&A.
* **10:00 - 11:30 | Schematic Capture**
  * Step-by-step creation of the circuit schematic by participants.
* **11:30 - 12:00 | Morning Wrap-up**
  * Open doubt session and buffer/grace time.

* **12:00 - 14:00 | Lunch Break**

### Session 2: Layout, Routing & Manufacturing
* **14:00 - 14:45 | Signal Integrity & Electromagnetics**
  * Understanding EMI, crosstalk, signal reflections, and ground bounce.
  * How physics directly influences physical board layouts.

* **14:45 - 16:30 | PCB Layout & Routing**
  * Component placement strategy.
  * Routing tracks, managing layer stacks, and applying best practices.
  * Troubleshooting layout and routing issues.

* **16:30 - 17:00 | Manufacturing, Cost Optimization and Closing QnA**
  * Generating Gerber files and Bill of Materials (BOM).
  * How to order from manufacturers (linking back to sponsors).
  * Factors driving up PCB fabrication costs.
  * Doubt session
